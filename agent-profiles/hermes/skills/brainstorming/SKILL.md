---
name: brainstorming
description: Explore multiple approaches and stress-test them before committing. Use at the start of any non-trivial design or decision.
---

## When to use
Before committing to a design, plan, or significant decision.

## Procedure
1. Generate at least two or three genuinely different approaches.
2. For each, note what it optimizes for and what it trades off, with evidence where possible.
3. Stress-test from an adversarial angle: how does each fail?
4. Pick one and state explicitly why, and what would change your mind.

## Pitfalls
- Anchoring on the first idea.
- Comparing options on tone instead of real outcomes/trade-offs.
- No falsifiable reason for the choice.

## Verification
- At least two real alternatives were considered.
- Trade-offs are explicit.
- The decision has a stated rationale and a 'what would change my mind'.
